Problems Identified

Important hygiene-related concerns observed during our project.

Improper Waste Disposal

Throwing garbage in open areas makes the surroundings dirty and can create health risks.

Unclean Surroundings

Accumulated waste can attract flies and pests and reduce environmental cleanliness.

Hygiene Awareness

Awareness about handwashing, safe water, sanitation and cleanliness is important.

Healthy Practices

Simple practices that can be followed every day.

🧼 Wash Hands

Wash hands regularly with soap, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

💧 Safe Water

Keep drinking water in clean and covered containers.

🚻 Proper Sanitation

Keep toilets and surrounding areas clean.

♻️ Proper Waste Disposal

Dispose of waste responsibly and follow proper waste segregation practices.
